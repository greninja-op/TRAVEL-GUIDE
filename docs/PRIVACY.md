# Privacy & Permissions (`travel-guide/docs/PRIVACY.md`)

User-facing copy doubles as the first-launch notice (SPEC §1).

## What we collect — and why

- **Location (foreground):** to know which POI you're at. Asked once, with a
  plain-language rationale screen before the system dialog.
- **Location (background):** ONLY if you enable it in Settings for
  walk-mode narration with the screen off. Separate opt-in, revocable, and
  the app fully works in foreground-only mode.
- **No account, no tracking SDKs.** Visits, notes and photos stay on-device.
  Trip share/export creates a file you choose to send — nothing uploads itself.

## Battery

GPS costs power. The app shows the active profile (Precise / Balanced /
Saver — Phase 3) in Settings and in the persistent notification, and never
runs high-accuracy background polling without telling you.

## Content honesty

History vs legend vs fun-fact are labeled on every card; every POI shows its
sources + pack version. Folklore is never presented as fact.
