Credits & provenance

What this page is made of

Photography · map data · icons · type · app design system

Photography

The Fort Kochi photographs on this site come from the Travel Guide project’s own app assets (mobile/app/src/main/res/drawable/), including the Chinese fishing nets, St. Francis Church, Jew Town, the beach, the jetty, and the Dutch Cemetery. They are used here to show the real places the guide covers. Replace or re-license them before a public commercial launch if your rights review requires it.

Map data

The map on the phone mockup is a static crop of OpenStreetMap tiles around Fort Kochi, zoom 16. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). The live app uses MapLibre Native with its own bundled style and tiles.

Icons

All interface glyphs are from Lucide, used as a single outline family at 24px with a 2px stroke, round caps and joins. Lucide is licensed under the ISC License. No emoji, no mixed icon sets, no generated glyphs.

Type

Archivo (display), Inter (body and app UI), Instrument Serif (editorial italic accent), and DM Mono (labels and metrics) are self-hosted from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Lora (app stories) is bundled from the Android project, also under the SIL OFL 1.1.

App design system

The phone screens reproduce the shipped Travel Guide tokens and components: coral #FF5A36, midnight carbon #121826, amber #F59E0B, Inter + Lora, 18px cards, 14px buttons, 24px sheets, and the 5-tab Map / Nearby / Packs / History / Settings dock. The canonical reference is design-reference/reference.html in the repository.

Content

Stop names, distances, opening hours, route order, and story lines are drawn from the Fort Kochi pack v1.1.0 (content/packs/fort-kochi-walk-v1.json) and the app’s shipped strings. The page avoids invented review scores, user counts, and testimonials.
